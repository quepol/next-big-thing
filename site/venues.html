What it shows

  • Each SF room has a recognisable Toronto twin, at a similar size. Café du Nord → the Drake Underground and Drake Hotel. Bottom of the Hill → Monarch Tavern, Sneaky Dee's and the Baby G. The Independent → Velvet Underground, the Mod Club and Longboat Hall. Great American Music Hall → the Great Hall, the Concert Hall and the Phoenix. Bimbo's, the Fillmore and the Regency → the Concert Hall, the Danforth and the Opera House.
  • Much of the overlap is the touring circuit. Around 60% of the artists at most of your rooms also played Toronto. The rooms at the top of the city-wide ranking (the Concert Hall, the Danforth, the Opera House) rank high because they book national touring acts, like your SF rooms do. Bottom of the Hill is the exception: only 31% of its artists reached Toronto, which makes it your most local and most exploratory room.
  • Rooms to watch: the Drake Underground, Monarch Tavern, Velvet Underground, the Great Hall and Longboat Hall are the closest small-room matches. About 445 Bottom of the Hill artists and 97 Café du Nord artists haven't played Toronto since Oct 2023, so they're worth knowing when they do.
